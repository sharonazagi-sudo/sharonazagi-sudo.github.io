Selected works
2020

Hammer

Five-channel synchronised video mapping, pulse sensor, pyrotechnics, concrete blocks

A structure built near the Egyptian border, its blocks packed with forty-five charges. A sensor reads the artist's pulse; one charge fires every six heartbeats. The more it collapses, the faster her heart beats — and the faster it collapses.
2024

Turbulence

Tesla coils, fluorescent tubes, balloons, pyrotechnics, two-channel video, voice, custom electronics

A song performed to an audience of locusts in an abandoned desert greenhouse. Each time the syllable s occurs, a fuse ignites and a balloon releases and burns. In the gallery, Tesla coils play the instrumental part by discharging into suspended fluorescent tubes at frequencies close to those of the insects.
2022

Atlas II

Live stream, sound, neural network, custom software, projection, mixed media

Software reads the live solar feed from NASA's SDO observatory — ray frequencies, surface oscillations, the sun's position relative to the earth — and a neural network composes from them an organ-like score. A sundial that keeps time without announcing an hour.
2025

A Shell of a House

Video projected onto vapour, vapour generator, ducting, pump, fan, custom software

A recording of the artist's late grandmother singing drives every device in the room. Her face is projected onto vapour that issues from the ducting only while she sings, so the figure she is made of disperses each time her breath fails.
2016–2019

Exhibits

Video, infinite loops

Endless loops drawn from four orders of material — a fruit lifted from Bosch, a plant, a living organism recorded under magnification, solar video from NASA — each subjected to the same treatment until it arrives at the same condition. Shown here as the opening of this site.
