A Shell of a House

2025 · The Lab, Tel Aviv
Curated by Sharon Toval
Biosensor, sweet potato plant, projection, vapour, custom software
A Shell of a House · The Lab, 2025

A living organism is wired as a witness.

A biosensor attached to a sweet potato plant registers its vital electrical activity and converts it, in real time, into the respiration of the artist's late grandmother — projected onto the wall and released as vapour through a length of ducting. The dead woman breathes for as long as the plant keeps producing a signal.

The room around the projection is assembled from domestic residue: a figure in protective coveralls collapsed against an electric fan, a field of knives set in plaster, citrus suspended in sealed bags, a clock whose face has been overgrown. Nothing is performed. The system runs on its own, and the household it reconstructs continues without anyone present to maintain it.
