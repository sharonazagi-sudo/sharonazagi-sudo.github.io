Turbulence

2024 · Alfred Gallery, Tel Aviv
Curated by Edith Kopski and Bar Musan Levi
Tesla coils, fluorescent tubes, balloons, pyrotechnics, two-channel video, voice, custom electronics
Turbulence · Single-channel video
Exhibition view · Alfred Gallery, 2024

A song performed for an audience of locusts.

In an abandoned desert greenhouse at night, the artist stands and sings. Her voice has been processed through a profile built from locust calls, and the sound is addressed to them rather than to any listener present. Every time the syllable s occurs in the lyric, it triggers an ignition: a fuse catches at the base of one balloon, burns up the line, and the balloon releases into the air and burns as it goes. One at a time, for the length of the song.

Inside the gallery the band is the room. Tesla coils suspended along fluorescent tubes discharge into the glass and light it without contact, each group taking a part of the arrangement — drums here, bass there, lead guitar across the space — at frequencies that sit close to those of the insects. At the far end, a small close-up projection holds the singing alone. The circuit performs the accompaniment; the recording carries the voice. Neither requires a visitor to continue.
