About

Sharon Azagi (b. 1986) is a new media artist and a certified forensic expert in the authentication of audio, video and image evidence. Her installations extend the procedures of forensic examination toward phenomena that admit no admissible proof — the electrical activity of plants, the frequencies of the sun, the rhythm of a heart.

She builds systems, partly physical and partly computational, that take one kind of information and convert it into another. These systems are designed to function on their own and for themselves, without human involvement — breathing, pulsating organisms that act autonomously and repetitively as long as electricity flows through their veins. Between the digital and the physical, the objects she produces expose the vital and mental forces latent in matter, light, water and sound.

Solo exhibitions include A Shell of a House (The Lab, 2025), Turbulence (Alfred Gallery, 2024) and Atlas II (Petah Tikva Museum of Art, 2022). Her work has been shown at the WRO Media Art Biennale (Poland), Haifa Museum of Art, the Municipal Gallery Arsenał (Poznań) and BETONBOX (Düsseldorf). She holds an MFA from the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design and lives and works in Israel.
